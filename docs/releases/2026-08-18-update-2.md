# CODE Portal, Update 2

A smaller, corrective update. The theme is fixing attendance after the fact: taking it for a room full of people at once, and undoing it when someone is marked present by mistake. Points also stop being whole numbers.

---

## Taking attendance

**Check in a whole list at once.** Alongside searching for one member at a time, you can now paste a column of emails — straight out of a spreadsheet — and check everyone in together. Up to 500 per batch.

The result tells you exactly what happened rather than a single number: how many were checked in, how many were already present, and — the one that matters — **which emails matched nobody**, listed back so you can fix a typo or find the person who never signed up. Invalid entries and any individual failures are listed separately too.

Pasting the same list twice is safe. The second run reports everyone as already present and awards nothing again.

**Remove someone marked present by mistake.** Every name in the Present list now has a remove button. Previously the only way to undo a check-in was the Undo button that appears for a moment after scanning — once it was gone, so was your chance.

Removing a check-in also removes any points it awarded. A member cannot keep credit for an event they were taken off.

**You can see when something is working.** Marking a member present now shows that it is in progress rather than sitting silent until the name appears. The bulk button does the same and locks the box while it runs.

**Removals ask properly.** Taking someone off attendance now opens a dialog that spells out that points go too, replacing the small browser prompt that cut its own message short on a phone.

---

## Points

**Points can have decimals.** An event can be worth 0.75 retention points, or 1.25, or 2.5. Everything that adds points up — totals, history, exports — handles fractions, and values are kept to two decimal places so nothing displays as 0.7500000001.

Existing whole-number points are unaffected and read back exactly as before.

Milestone targets and the retained/probation thresholds stay whole numbers. A target of "100 points" works the same against a fractional total.

---

## Your points page — behind a switch

This update also contains a member-facing points page and a rebuilt progress display. **Both stay hidden on the live site until the retention switch is turned on**, so nothing below appears for members the day this ships.

When it is switched on, members get:

**A points page** showing their own history for the term.

**A milestone track.** Every milestone sits on one rail with the reached ones filled, and a single line saying how far the next one is — "4.25 to Silver". It replaces a bar that measured against the final target, so progress through the tier you are actually in is now what you see.

**Points on the calendar.** Days where points were earned show the amount in the calendar cell. A Heatmap toggle switches to shaded cells for reading a month at a glance, with a scale showing what the darkest shade is worth. Exact figures stay available to screen readers and on hover in both modes.

The leaderboard is deliberately switched off separately, so the points page can be shown without a ranking table.

---

## Fixes worth calling out

- **The remove button no longer washes out when you hover it.** It stayed muted because the button style underneath it overrode the hover colour.
- **Checking someone in no longer looks like nothing happened** while the request is in flight.
- **Editing a pasted list mid-submission** can no longer disagree with the count shown on the button.

---

## Under the hood

Nothing here changes what you see, but it is why the update is safe to ship.

- The points columns changed from whole numbers to decimals. Two tables were rebuilt; both were checked to have nothing depending on them first, and the change is lossless for every value already stored.
- The migration was rehearsed on beta and on the staging copy of production before production was offered it.
- Bulk check-in reuses the same code path as a physical scan, so the check-in window rules, the points award, the duplicate protection and the audit log are identical whether a member is scanned, searched for, or pasted in.
- A pasted list is restricted to event admins. A scanner assigned to an event still checks members in one at a time.
- The test suite grew from 488 to 511 tests.
